How it works

If someone forces you to log in, give them this password instead of your real one. It opens your cover files, a vault of its own where everything works, uploads and sharing included, and quietly warns the administrator. Your other files stay out of its reach: they are locked by keys only your real password opens. With the wipe option it first destroys those other files, and nobody can get them back, not even you.

A new duress password starts to work {{ delay }} after it is saved, so whoever watches you set one cannot try it on the spot. Saving a new one replaces the old one at once. This page never shows whether a duress password is set.

Never let your browser save the duress password, here or after a login: it could later fill it in for you by mistake.
